Questions

Good to know before we start.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. This portfolio is designed to adapt to phones, tablets, and desktop screens. Each client website should also be tested on common screen sizes before launch.

Does the price include a domain and hosting?

Not by default. Domain registration and hosting can have separate costs. The final quote should state clearly what is included and who owns the accounts.

Can you add online payments or bookings?

These features may be possible, but they require extra planning and sometimes third-party services, secure configuration, or a backend. They are not included in the basic packages by default.

How do I get started?

Use the enquiry form to send your project details through WhatsApp. We'll need to agree on scope, cost, timeline, and content before work begins.
